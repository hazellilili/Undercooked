## 步骤说明

1. **Prep the fish 杀鱼**
   
   Slice the fish filet thinly and marinate them briefly with black pepper, salt, and ginger. The classic method uses a whole fish for stock, and the slices are usually marinated with egg white and blablabla, but I am lazy, so I use frozen fish filet directly. 
   
   鱼排片薄，用黑胡椒盐姜腌制一下。一般做法是用整鱼吊汤，鱼片要用蛋清blablabla腌制，我懒，直接用冷冻鱼排。

2. **Cooking the soup 煮制**

    Stir-fry the ginger and garlic until fragrant, then add the sauerkraut and keep frying until it is dry and aromatic. Add boiling water, then season with a little white pepper, salt, and 4 tablespoons of white vinegar. Put the toppings like tofu in first and simmer for about 20 minutes to bring out the flavor of sauerkraut and also let the toppings suck the flavor. Once the broth tastes right, remove the toppings and sauerkraut, then add the fish slices and cook until just done. Pour the soup and fish over the sauerkraut. 
    
    用姜蒜爆锅，放酸菜炒干炒香。加开水后加调料，一点白胡椒和盐，四勺白醋。先把配菜放进去煮，汤需要煮二十分钟左右才能出味道。出味道后把配菜和酸菜捞出，再下鱼片滚熟。连汤带鱼浇在配菜上。

3. **Finishing with chili oil 泼油**

    Heat the oil until it smokes. You can either add the oil ingredients to the hot oil and quickly pour the whole over the fish, or sprinkle the ingredients over the fish first and then pour hot oil on top. I personally prefer the first method. If you add boiling water immediately on the frying sauerkraut, the soup becomes very cloudy and thick. In the end, the Sichuan pepper aroma is the finishing touch and makes it absolutely perfect with rice. 
    
    把油烧冒烟，要么把泼油料倒进油里后迅速浇再鱼肉上，要么把泼油料撒再鱼上再往上浇热油，我个人更喜欢前者。酸菜炒后直接加开水会让汤很白很浓，最后花椒的香味非常点睛，无敌下饭!
